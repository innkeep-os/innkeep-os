# Learnings about you

`- YYYY-MM-DD [high|medium|low] the observation`._

_**Things seen ONCE, never a queue.** When a new observation confirms one that
is already here, that is the second sighting: it gets promoted into
`preferences.md` or `profile.md` with both dates and the evidence, and the
staged line is deleted — in the same turn, by the session holding the evidence.
