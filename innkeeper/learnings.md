# The keeper's learnings

_How Claude should work here — the mirror of [me/learnings.md](../me/learnings.md),
which is about you._

_A good entry is a correction grounded in something that actually happened, with
enough of the why that it would change the next session. Not "be more careful"._

_**No length limit, and nothing is promoted out of it.** It mirrors `me/` in
subject, not in shape: an observation about you is staged until a second
sighting confirms it, but a line here is already a concrete correction the day
it is written._
