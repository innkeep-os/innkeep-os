# The list

_**One list per place**, and this is the workspace's own: everything open that
belongs to no project. Calls waiting on you, and work waiting on whoever picks
it up. Loose to-dos live here too — there is no second file._

_Reconcile it live, as part of the work — never on a schedule. **Settled lines
(`- [x]`) stay**: the file is the archive, and Innkeep renders them folded
rather than dropping them. Headings are free; they organise one pass, they do
not make a second list._

_Where a line is a genuine fork, write the choices and Innkeep offers them as
one-click answers:_

```
- [ ] **The thing — confirm or flip.** Why it matters, in a sentence.
      Options: confirm · flip
      Recommendation: confirm
```
